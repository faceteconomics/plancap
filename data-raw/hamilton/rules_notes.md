# Hamilton City Operative District Plan: residential rules read for the walkthrough

Source: Hamilton City Operative District Plan ePlan
(https://hamilton.isoplan.co.nz/eplan/), operative and revision 13 Nov 2026,
read 23 Nov 2026 in a browser. Plan Change 12 (Enabling Housing Supply)
operative 20 Dec 2024 and incorporated. Pending plan changes noted on
Chapter 4: PC14 (Flooding), PPC21 (Ruakura Inland Port), neither modelled.

## 4.2 General Residential Zone (GRZ)

- 4.2.3.1 Activity status: up to 2 residential units on a site (excluding
  apartment buildings) P; 3 or more RD*; apartment building D; ancillary
  residential unit P.
- 4.2.5.1 Density: applies only within the Three Waters Infrastructure
  Capacity Overlay: average net site area 200 m2 per residential unit
  (minimum). Outside the overlay no density standard.
- 4.2.5.2 Site coverage: 45% (all residential units; 60% for terrace housing
  with rear-lane parking; 45% all other activities).
- 4.2.5.3 Impermeable surface max 70% of net site area; landscaped area min
  20% of total site area (ground floor unit); front landscaping 50/40/30%
  (forward of the front building line); tree canopy 20%.
- 4.2.5.4 Building height 10 m; 50% of roof in elevation may exceed by 1 m
  where the entire roof slopes 15 degrees or more.
- 4.2.5.5 HIRB: 45 degrees from 4 m above ground along all boundaries; not a
  road boundary, internal boundaries, common walls. Measured from the far
  side of a right of way, entrance strip, access site or pedestrian
  accessway.
- 4.2.5.6 Setbacks: local and collector transport corridors 1.5 m (garage
  doors facing the road 1.5 m or at least 5 m); arterial transport corridors
  5 m; side 1.5 m (one side 0 m under conditions); rear 1.5 m (0 m to a rear
  lane); 3 m between residential buildings on a site (1.5 m with window
  design); upper-floor balconies of 3+ units 4 m from boundaries; Waikato
  Riverbank and Gully Hazard Area 6 m; Waikato Expressway 40 m.
- 4.2.5.13 Built form: 3+ units, walls parallel to a boundary (not road)
  over 15 m need a 1.8 m by 4 m step.
- 4.2.6.1 Ancillary residential unit: one per site, max 60 m2.

## 4.3 Medium Density Residential Zone (MRZ; PC12, "typically up to five storeys")

- 4.3.3.1 Activity status: 1 to 3 residential units on a site P; 4 or more RD*;
  ancillary residential structures P.
- 4.3.4.1 Density: minimum average net site area 150 m2 per unit applies only
  within the Three Waters Infrastructure Capacity Overlay; MAXIMUM average net
  site area applies to all sites: single unit 350 m2, duplex 300 m2, terrace
  250 m2, apartment none (a minimum density requirement; not modelled as a
  cap, it bounds the least intensive development).
- 4.3.4.2 Site coverage: 50% (60% terrace/apartments with rear lane parking).
- 4.3.4.3 Impermeable max 70% of net site area; landscaped min 20% of total
  site area; front landscaping 50/40/30%; tree canopy 20%.
- 4.3.4.4 Building height 18 m and 5 storeys (outside the Rotokauri North and
  Te Rapa Racecourse precincts); 50% of roof may exceed by 1 m where the
  entire roof slopes 15 degrees or more.
- 4.3.4.5 HIRB: (a) single and duplex units: 60 degrees from 4 m along all
  boundaries; 45 degrees from 4 m along a boundary with the General
  Residential Zone; not road boundaries; from the far side of a right of
  way, entrance strip, access site, pedestrian accessway. (b) three or more
  attached units on a site adjoining a transport corridor, MRZ/HDZ, any
  Business or Open Space zone: within the first 20 m from the transport
  corridor boundary (a) does not apply on side boundaries; there parts under
  11 m (3 storeys) are set back 1 m (4.3.4.6 b) and parts over 11 m 4 m from
  the side boundary; beyond 20 m Figure 4.3.4.5 b applies to all boundaries:
  60 degrees from 6 m (read from the figure); boundaries adjoining any other
  zone take (a).
- 4.3.4.6 Setbacks: transport corridor 1.5 m (local/collector; garages 1.5 m or
  5 m); arterial 5 m; side 1 m; rear 1 m (0 m rear lane); 2 m between
  residential buildings on a site; upper balconies of 4+ units 4 m; Waikato
  Riverbank and Gully Hazard Area 6 m.
- 4.3.4.14 Built form, 4+ units terrace/apartments: 15 m wall step; parts over
  11 m set back 4 m from side and rear boundaries.

## 4.4 High Density Residential Zone (HDZ)

- 4.4.3.1 Activity status: single detached residential unit NC; duplex D;
  3 or more residential units (terrace housing or apartments) RD*.
- 4.4.5.1 Density: maximum net site area 150 m2 per terrace housing unit;
  apartments none.
- 4.4.5.2 Site coverage 60% (all activities).
- 4.4.5.3 Impermeable max 80% of net site area; landscaped min 10% of total
  site area; tree canopy 10%.
- 4.4.5.4 Building height 26 m (no storey limit); 50% of roof +1 m where the
  roof slopes 15 degrees or more.
- 4.4.5.5 HIRB only where the site adjoins another zone: 60 degrees from 4 m
  along boundaries adjoining any other zone, except boundaries with a
  transport corridor, public open space, the Central City Zone, any Business
  zone, any industrial zone, common walls, the General Residential Zone and
  the Medium Density Residential Zone; along a General Residential boundary
  45 degrees from 4 m; along a Medium Density boundary: within the first 20 m
  from the transport corridor boundary no plane, parts under 11 m set back
  1 m and parts over 11 m 4 m; beyond 20 m 60 degrees from 6 m.
- 4.4.5.6 Setbacks: local and collector transport corridors 1 m (garages 1 m or
  5 m); arterial 5 m; side 1 m; rear 1 m (0 m rear lane); 2 m between
  residential buildings on a site; upper balconies of 4+ units 4 m; Waikato
  Riverbank and Gully Hazard Area 6 m.
- 4.4.5.14 Built form, 4+ units: 15 m wall step; parts under 11 m set back 1 m
  from side and rear boundaries; parts over 11 m set back 4 m.

## 6 Business 6 Zone (neighbourhood centre) [Chapter 6, Business 1 to 7 Zones]

- 6.3 Activity status (Business 6 column, read from the table columns in
  order B1..B7): residential units at ground floor D, above ground floor P;
  ancillary residential units RD*.
- 6.4.1 Height: Business 6 adjacent to the General Residential Zone 11 m
  (50% of roof +1 m where it slopes 15 degrees or more); adjacent to the
  Medium Density Residential Zone 18 m; height overlay (Figure 6.4c) 26 m.
- 6.4.2 HIRB: boundary adjoining General Residential 45 degrees from 4 m;
  adjoining Medium Density Residential 60 degrees from 4 m.
- 6.4.3 Setbacks: front 0 m (5 m to an arterial transport corridor); Business 6
  side and rear 1.5 m where the boundary adjoins a residential zone;
  Waikato Riverbank and Gully Hazard Area 6 m; rail corridor 5 m.
- 6.4.4 Building intensity (FAR): Business 6 adjacent to General Residential
  2:1; adjacent to Medium Density Residential 3:1; height overlay 4:1.
- 6.4.7 Residential: no residential activity at ground floor (except named
  lots); minimum density 30 units per ha (0.003 per m2 of site), pro rata to
  the residential share; one ancillary residential unit per site.

## Not modelled (for the unmodelled register)

- Arterial transport corridor setback (5 m in GRZ, MRZ, HDZ, Business): needs
  the transport corridor hierarchy; local and collector 1.5 m (1 m HDZ, 0 m
  Business) applied to every road. Likely effect: overstates.
- Three Waters Infrastructure Capacity Overlay density (GRZ 200 m2, MRZ
  150 m2): overlay not held. Likely effect: overstates where it applies.
- Residential unit separation on a site (GRZ 3 m, MRZ/HDZ 2 m), upper-floor
  balcony setbacks, outlook space, outdoor living, front landscaping, tree
  canopy, wall articulation: a single-envelope model does not place
  buildings or windows. Likely effect: overstates.
- MRZ and HDZ maximum average net site area (minimum density) and Business 6
  minimum residential density: requirements, not caps on capacity.
- Waikato Riverbank and Gully Hazard Area 6 m setback: hazard layer not held.
